About the hotel

A convenient base in Dholanwal Sodhiwal

Taj Palace Hotel (listed on Google Maps as New Taj Palace Hotel) sits on Bund Road, directly opposite the City Bus Terminal and near Rahber/Bilal Travel — a practical pick if you're arriving by bus or heading out early the next morning. It's rated a 2-star budget hotel, built for travelers who want a clean, affordable room without the extras of a full-service hotel.

The hotel keeps things simple: air-conditioned rooms, breakfast, free Wi-Fi, free parking for those driving in, a pool, and laundry service — the essentials covered, at a rate that suits a short overnight stop or a longer budget stay in Lahore.
